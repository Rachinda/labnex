Embedded, Automation & Smart Control Solutions

We design and deploy custom embedded control circuits, PLC/SCADA/HMI programming, and fully integrated industrial automation systems tailored to unique operational needs.

Our solutions include:

  • Timers, counters, queue-management systems, wireless displays, PC-controlled interfaces
  • Industrial-grade battery and mobile power backup systems
  • Control panel design, legacy system retrofitting, dependable after-sales technical support

Calibration, Testing & Certification Services

We provide ISO 17025:2017 NABL-accredited calibration services across mechanical, thermal, electrotechnical, and laboratory instruments—ensuring traceability and measurement accuracy.

Our services cover:

  • Testing of textile equipment, hardness testers, viscometers, fabric testers, precision instruments
  • Validation services: cleanroom, product, autoclave
  • Certification support: ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 27001
  • Environmental control: temperature & humidity mapping for warehouses
