    Our Story

    LABNEX is a Sri Lankan-based supplier and distributor dedicated to supplying high quality laboratory equipment and consumables tailored to the textile sector. Built on a foundation of trust, precision, and customer-first service, LABNEX serves textile testing laboratories, apparel manufacturers, export houses, research institutes, and quality control departments across Sri Lanka and beyond.

    • Focused on lab essentials built for the textile sector
    • Trusted by quality control departments and export houses
    • Committed to reliable supply, service, and expansion

    Currently specializing in the local supply and distribution of instruments and consumables, LABNEX is setting its sights on the future—with upcoming plans to expand into chemical imports and become a leading lab partner in the South Asian region.
