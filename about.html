About LABNEX

LABNEX is a trusted provider of high-quality laboratory materials and testing supplies, serving textile, garment, and industrial clients with a strong commitment to quality and customer satisfaction. We are based in Colombo and deliver across Sri Lanka and beyond. Our slogan, "High-quality items are the right choice," reflects our mission to supply the most reliable and certified products to laboratories and testing environments.

Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative partner for laboratory solutions in Sri Lanka’s textile sector and beyond, recognised for delivering world-class quality, reliability, and value across equipment, consumables, and technical services.

Mission

To provide accurate, standard-compliant laboratory equipment, consumables, and technical support that meet international standards, while offering expertise in automation, calibration, testing, and consultancy. We are dedicated to enhancing client efficiency, ensuring compliance, and building lasting relationships with customers and global suppliers through exceptional service and innovative solutions.

Brands

Our carefully selected range of products reflects our commitment to delivering only the best to our customers. Whether you’re looking for cutting-edge technology, timeless design, or proven performance, our brands represent the highest standards in their industries — ensuring you always get products you can trust.
